Research

Research

My research centers on computational methods for tomographic image reconstruction, with current interests in X-ray computed tomography and single-photon emission computed tomography.

Computed Tomography

CT Reconstruction

I am interested in model-based and learning-assisted approaches for reconstructing high-quality CT images from measured projection data. My work broadly explores inverse problems, statistical reconstruction, image priors, and computational methods for improving reconstruction quality and efficiency.

Nuclear Medicine Imaging

SPECT Reconstruction

I am also interested in quantitative SPECT reconstruction, including physics-aware forward models and statistical reconstruction methods for photon-counting measurements. The broader goal is to develop reliable computational imaging methods that account for the measurement process while producing accurate reconstructed activity distributions.

Broader Interests

Computational Imaging & Scientific Machine Learning

More broadly, I am interested in computational imaging, inverse problems, optimization, uncertainty-aware reconstruction, and machine-learning methods that can complement physics-based imaging models.
