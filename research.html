Statistical Model-Based Reconstruction for SPECT

Developing scalable reconstruction approaches for quantitative SPECT, accounting for measurement statistics and imaging-system physics to pursue accurate and reliable three-dimensional imaging.

Poisson MBIRMAP estimationqGGMRFGPU projectors
Explore technical focus

Maximum-likelihood reconstruction with MLEM and OSEM; Poisson statistics; MAP estimation; qGGMRF regularization; collimator-detector response; point spread functions; attenuation modeling; system matrices; GPU forward and backprojection; comparison against existing reconstruction methods.

Diffusion Priors & Plug-and-Play Reconstruction

Exploring how learned image distributions can complement physical measurement models for reconstruction from noisy or incomplete observations.

Denoising diffusion probabilistic models, score-based generative modeling, diffusion-prior-assisted optimization, physics-informed inverse problems, and Bayesian interpretations of learned image priors. This is an ongoing investigation; validated improvements are not claimed.
